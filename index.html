About FDOWN

FDOWN is a web-based Facebook video downloader designed around a simple URL workflow. The page provides a clear entry point for users who want to process supported, publicly available video links in a modern browser.

What the tool is designed to do

The service is intended to help users process supported Facebook video URLs and access available download options. Results can vary depending on the source, video availability, technical changes and access permissions.

Who this page is for

This page is useful for people who need a simple browser-based workflow for saving content they own, created, are licensed to use, or otherwise have permission to download.

Important usage information

  • Respect copyright, privacy and platform rules.
  • Only download content when you have the necessary rights or permission.
  • Do not use the service to bypass access restrictions or obtain content you are not authorized to access.
  • Download availability and quality depend on the supported source.
Rights and permissions matter.
FDOWN does not claim ownership of third-party content. You are responsible for ensuring that your use of downloaded material is lawful and authorized.

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers to common questions about using the downloader.

How do I use FDOWN?

Copy a supported public Facebook video link, paste it into the tool at the top of this page, and select an available download option.

Do I need to install an application?

No separate application is required for the basic browser-based workflow. Use a modern compatible browser.

Does FDOWN work on mobile devices?

The page is responsive and intended to be used on modern mobile and desktop browsers. The final download behavior may vary by browser and device.

Can every Facebook video be downloaded?

No. Availability depends on technical support, accessibility, permissions and the source itself. Restricted or unsupported content may not be available.

Is FDOWN associated with Facebook or Meta?

No. FDOWN is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Facebook or Meta.
